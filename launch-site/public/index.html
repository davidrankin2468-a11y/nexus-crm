COMMON QUESTIONS

Know before you get started.

Can I try Nexus CRM without creating an account?

Yes. The public interactive demo works with fictional browser-based data. The separate private cloud application requires an authorized account.

Is Nexus CRM already available to purchase?

Not yet. We are collecting early-access interest while completing security, onboarding, billing, off-site backups, and final quality assurance.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The browser preview is designed for mobile and desktop use. Native app stores and fully offline synchronization are not yet part of this release.

Will it work for my trade?

The CRM is being designed for landscaping, painting, construction and other field services. You can request industry-specific adjustments through the early-access form.

Does it collect or process payments?

It can record payments that were received outside the CRM. The alpha does not charge cards, transfer money, or connect to a payment processor.
