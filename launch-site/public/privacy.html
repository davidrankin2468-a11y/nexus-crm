HOW EARLY-ACCESS INQUIRIES ARE HANDLED

Privacy notice

Effective date: November 16, 2026. This notice covers the public Nexus CRM product website and its early-access inquiry form—not the separate authenticated CRM service.

The public demo is for fictional data only. Do not enter your customers' private information, passwords, financial account details, or payment card information.

Who operates the site?

The product website is operated under the Nexus Software Solutions name. It is a pre-launch software project. Business contact and legal-entity details will be finalized before commercial release.

What we collect

If you submit an early-access request, we collect your email address, optional business name, selected industry, optional note, your explicit consent, and timestamps needed to review the inquiry. We also process a hashed network identifier to limit spam; the form does not store your raw IP address in its inquiry database.

How we use information

We use it only to review your product inquiry, contact you about Nexus CRM early access, and prioritize product development needs. We do not sell inquiry information, process payments through this site, or automatically create a cloud CRM account.

Where information is kept

Inquiry records are stored in a separate Cloudflare D1 database. Website traffic is served by Cloudflare, which may process routine technical and security logs under its own infrastructure policies. Inquiry details are not placed in the publicly accessible GitHub repository or in the interactive demo.

Access, retention, and deletion

Inquiry details are intended for project-owner review. They remain until manually removed, your withdrawal request is fulfilled, or records are cleaned up as part of site administration. There is no guaranteed automatic expiry for submitted inquiries yet.

When you first submit a request, you receive a private reference code. You can use that code to remove your stored inquiry on the withdrawal page. If you lose the code, please submit a fresh inquiry explaining the removal request so it can be reviewed manually.

Security and changes

The website uses HTTPS, basic submission limits and private database access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If we materially change what the form collects or how it is used, we will update this notice before expanding collection.

Do not treat this notice as a finalized legal privacy policy for the future paid CRM. Separate product-account terms, data-processing agreements and retention rules must be reviewed before commercial onboarding.
